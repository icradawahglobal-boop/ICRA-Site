About ICRA

Working through universities, the media, and local communities with respect for everyone we meet.

Why we do this

In much of Sub-Saharan Africa, Muslims are a small minority and Islamic literature is hard to find. Too often, people learn about Islam from those who are not Muslim, and misunderstandings follow. We want more Muslims to speak for themselves, and we want to give people the chance to hear about Islam directly.

How we work

We hold lectures, workshops, and training programmes with university students and scholars. We speak on national media in each country we visit, distribute Islamic literature where it is scarce, and connect with callers to Islam who are already working on the ground.
